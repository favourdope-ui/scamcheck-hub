Privacy Notice

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This notice explains how ScamCheck Hub handles information when you use the current public version of the service.

In short: message text is sent to our Supabase-hosted analysis endpoint to check for common warning signs. The app is designed to store a one-way fingerprint and the result, not the original message text, in its checks table. Do not paste passwords, one-time codes, recovery phrases, financial account details, or other highly sensitive information.

Information processed

  • Message checks: text you submit is sent to the analysis endpoint for rule-based processing. The application saves a SHA-256 fingerprint, score, risk label, and findings when database saving succeeds. The app does not intentionally put the original message in the check record.
  • Local check history: the browser may store up to 10 summaries (score, warning labels, time, and fingerprint) in local storage on that device. This history is not an account sync feature. Use the Clear local history button in the app or clear site data in your browser to remove it.
  • Email registrations: if you submit your email in the early-access form, we store the lower-case email address and registration time to count sign-ups and communicate launch updates. Email addresses are not publicly displayed. This form does not create a password-based account, and email ownership is not verified yet.
  • Scam reports: titles, targets, and descriptions you submit are stored so they can be reviewed. Reports are not displayed publicly unless their status is changed to published by an authorised moderator.
  • Profile pictures: the current picture tool previews the selected image and calculates a local fingerprint. It does not upload the selected image or perform reverse-image search.
  • Technical information: hosting and database providers may process technical request data required to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

Information is used to provide checks, save results, receive scam reports, protect the service, and improve reliability. We do not claim that a score proves a message or person is fraudulent or genuine.

Sharing and publication

We do not intentionally publish private check records. Scam reports may be displayed only after moderation changes their status to published. Do not include unnecessary personal information about yourself or another person in a report.

Retention and security

Local history remains in your browser until it is cleared or browser storage is removed. Database records are retained until an authorised deletion or retention process is implemented. We use database access policies to restrict access, but no internet service can promise perfect security.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit a message, report, or email registration. For removal of an email registration, contact the site operator through an officially published support channel when one is available; a self-service deletion feature is not yet implemented. Do not submit confidential information. Because account-based access and self-service deletion are not yet implemented, this version does not provide an in-app account or deletion dashboard.

Children and changes

This service is a general information tool and is not designed to collect information from children. This notice may change as the service develops; the date above identifies the current version.

Important limitation

ScamCheck Hub is an early-stage informational tool, not a certified security product, law-enforcement service, or financial adviser. Use independent verification before taking action.
