What to do if something feels wrong

Stop and verify

Contact the organisation through its official app or a number you find independently—not a link or number in the suspicious message.

Protect your accounts

Never share passwords, one-time codes, recovery phrases, or remote-access permissions with a stranger.

Pause payments

Be especially cautious with urgent transfers, gift cards, crypto, advance fees, or requests to keep a deal secret.

Keep evidence

Save relevant messages and transaction details securely. If money was sent, contact your bank or payment provider quickly.
